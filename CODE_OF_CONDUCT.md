# Code of Conduct

This project follows a blended Code of Conduct inspired by the **Contributor Covenant v2.1** and the **Code of Merit v1.0**.

## Principles
- Contributions are judged **solely on technical merit** and value to the project.
- All contributors are expected to **treat each other with respect and civility**.
- **Harassment, personal attacks, or discrimination of any kind are not tolerated.**
- Discussions must remain **focused on technical issues**; *politics, religion, or personal matters unrelated to the project are outside scope* — except where they directly involve the **safety or wellbeing of a contributor** in the context of the project.
- Mistakes are part of learning — contributors are given opportunities to improve and grow.

## Inclusivity
- We welcome participation from **people of all backgrounds, identities, and experiences.**
- We explicitly prohibit **discrimination based on race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic.**
- Our goal is to create a **safe, inclusive, and collaborative environment.**

## Enforcement
- Instances of **abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior** may be reported to the project maintainers.
- Maintainers may **warn, restrict, or remove contributors** whose behavior violates these standards.
- **Project owners and administrators have the final authority within the scope of this project** to interpret this Code of Conduct and decide on appropriate actions.
- Reports will be handled **confidentially and respectfully.**
- In cases involving **credible threats, illegal activity, or imminent physical danger**, maintainers are *not expected to mediate* — reports should be directed to appropriate local law enforcement or platform authorities (e.g., GitHub Trust & Safety) — [GitHub Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines).
- This project is also hosted on SourceForge. Platform-level conduct issues on that platform should be directed to [SourceForge support](https://sourceforge.net/support).

## Disclaimer
- This Code of Conduct is intended as a **community guideline, not a legal contract.**  
- This document does not limit or define the scope of **maintainer judgment** beyond what is stated in the Enforcement section above.  
- Participation in this project is **voluntary**, and contributors agree to abide by the *spirit of these rules.*  
- This document does not create **legal obligations or liabilities** for project maintainers beyond those required by applicable law.  
- This Code of Conduct does not supersede or replace the **platform’s own terms of service** (e.g., GitHub or SourceForge). Contributors must comply with both.
