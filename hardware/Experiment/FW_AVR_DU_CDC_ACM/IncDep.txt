  Direct-Unused:  0
  Maybe:          0

For all (includer -> included) pairs across the full chain:
  (counting only pairs where both files are in the project repo)

  Direct-Used:    ~35 pairs
  Direct-Unused:  ~25 pairs  (mainly aggregation umbrella headers and unused
                               sub-includes in system.h and usb_core_events.h)
  Transitive:     32 headers are purely transitive w.r.t. inc_usb_avr_du.h
  Maybe:          1  (usb_descriptors.h -> <stddef.h> re: wchar_t origin)

Notable observations:
  1. system.h is a pure aggregation umbrella: it includes 9 sub-headers but its
     own body only declares SYSTEM_Initialize(). Most of its sub-includes are
     DIRECT-UNUSED at the system.h level.
  2. usb_core_events.h aggregates 5 headers but its own body only declares
     USB_EventHandler(void). All 5 sub-includes are DIRECT-UNUSED at the
     usb_core_events.h level.
  3. usb_core_transfer.h has a self-include (#include <usb_core_transfer.h>
     inside usb_core_transfer.h itself, line 48) — this is a no-op due to the
     include guard but is an unusual pattern worth noting.
  4. usb0.h is a completely empty header (only include guard); it is
     DIRECT-UNUSED at every level that includes it.
  5. circular_buffer.h includes usb_common_elements.h unnecessarily; that
     include is DIRECT-UNUSED in circular_buffer.h's body.
  6. usb_peripheral_avr_du.h exists in the repo but is NOT reachable from
     inc_usb_avr_du.h via any include chain. It is used only by .c files.
  7. assembler.h / gas.h exist in the repo but are assembly-only headers
     guarded by __ASSEMBLER__ and are NOT reachable from C compilation of
     inc_usb_avr_du.h.
