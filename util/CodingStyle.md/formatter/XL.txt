by counting the `.` that is not part of languange construct (e.g., member access).

    // C++17/20/23 constructs: structured bindings, concepts/requires,
    // Consteval/constinit, <=>, coroutines, init-statement if/switch

As a matter a fact, there must be a list of to prevent the capitalization of reserved word such as
`consteval` above.
